# SGLT2 inhibitors and GLP-1 receptor agonists for kidney protection
Source: KDIGO 2022 Diabetes in CKD (Rec 1.3.1, 4.2.1); DAPA-CKD (NEJM 2020); CREDENCE (NEJM 2019); EMPA-KIDNEY (NEJM 2023); FLOW (NEJM 2024). Status: verify.

- SGLT2 inhibitor recommended for people with type 2 diabetes, CKD and eGFR 20 or more; continue even if eGFR later falls below 20, until dialysis or transplant.
- Kidney benefit in trials: dapagliflozin HR about 0.61 (DAPA-CKD), canagliflozin about 0.70 (CREDENCE) on kidney outcomes.
- Glucose-lowering effect is smaller when eGFR is low; kidney and heart benefits remain.
- Counsel on genital hygiene, sick-day rules (pause when not eating or drinking), and rare euglycaemic ketoacidosis.
- GLP-1 RA recommended when glucose targets are not met with metformin and SGLT2i, or when they cannot be used; semaglutide reduced kidney outcomes (FLOW, HR about 0.76) and helps weight loss. Common side effects are nausea and other GI symptoms.
