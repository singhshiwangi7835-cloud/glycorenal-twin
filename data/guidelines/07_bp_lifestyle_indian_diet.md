# Blood pressure, weight, activity and Indian meals
Source: KDIGO 2021 BP in CKD; ADA Standards of Care 2026 (sec 5, 10); WHO Expert Consultation, Lancet 2004 (Asian BMI cut-offs); ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024; Look AHEAD. Status: verify.

- BP target: below 130/80 for most people with diabetes and kidney disease (albuminuria); home BP checks help.
- Salt: under 5 g a day (about 1 teaspoon), including salt in pickles, papad and namkeen.
- Weight: for Indians, overweight starts at BMI 23. Losing 5% of body weight lowers HbA1c and BP.
- Activity: at least 150 minutes a week; a 10-15 minute walk after meals lowers the post-meal sugar rise. Each extra 2,000 steps a day helps.
- Indian meals: fill half the plate with vegetables, a quarter with protein (dal, paneer, egg, fish, chicken) and a quarter with cereals; choose millets (jowar, bajra, ragi), whole wheat or brown rice; smaller rice portions; avoid sugary drinks, sweets and fried snacks.
- Stopping smoking slows kidney disease.
