# CKD staging in diabetes (KDIGO)
Source: KDIGO 2024 CKD Evaluation and Management guideline; KDIGO 2022 Diabetes in CKD. Status: verify.

- CKD = eGFR below 60 mL/min/1.73m2 or albuminuria (urine albumin-creatinine ratio, UACR, 30 mg/g or more) lasting over 3 months.
- GFR categories: G1 90 or more, G2 60-89, G3a 45-59, G3b 30-44, G4 15-29, G5 below 15.
- Albuminuria categories: A1 below 30 mg/g, A2 30-300 mg/g, A3 above 300 mg/g.
- Risk of progression rises with both lower eGFR and higher albuminuria; the KDIGO heat map colours combinations green (low), yellow (moderate), orange (high), red (very high).
- People with type 2 diabetes should have eGFR (creatinine, CKD-EPI 2021 equation) and UACR checked at least once a year; more often when CKD is present.
- A single raised UACR should be confirmed with repeat tests because UACR varies from day to day.
