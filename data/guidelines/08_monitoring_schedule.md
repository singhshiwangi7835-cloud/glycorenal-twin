# What to check and how often
Source: ADA Standards of Care 2026 (sec 4, 11); KDIGO 2022 Diabetes in CKD. Status: verify.

- HbA1c: every 3 months if not at target or treatment changed; every 6 months if stable at target.
- Kidney: eGFR (creatinine) and UACR at least yearly; every 3-6 months when CKD is present or after starting ACEi/ARB, SGLT2i or finerenone.
- Potassium: with kidney tests, and 2-4 weeks after starting ACEi/ARB or finerenone.
- BP at every visit; home BP when not at target. Lipids yearly. Eye and foot checks yearly.
- Refer to a nephrologist when eGFR is below 30, UACR above 300 mg/g persistently, eGFR falls quickly (more than 5 per year), or potassium is hard to control.
