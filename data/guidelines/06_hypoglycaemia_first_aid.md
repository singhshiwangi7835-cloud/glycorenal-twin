# Low sugar (hypoglycaemia): what to do
Source: ADA Standards of Care 2026 (sec 6, hypoglycaemia); IDF / RSSDI patient guidance. Status: verify.

- Level 1: glucose below 70 mg/dL. Level 2: below 54 mg/dL. Level 3: needs help from another person.
- Symptoms: shaking, sweating, hunger, fast heartbeat, confusion.
- "15-15 rule": take 15 g of fast-acting sugar (3 teaspoons of sugar or glucose powder in water, or half a glass of fruit juice), wait 15 minutes, check again; repeat if still below 70. Then eat a snack or meal.
- If the person cannot swallow or is unconscious: do not give food by mouth; call emergency help.
- Tell the doctor about any low sugar, especially on sulfonylureas or insulin: the dose may need changing.
