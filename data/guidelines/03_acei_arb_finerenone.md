# Blocking the renin-angiotensin system: ACE inhibitor / ARB and finerenone
Source: KDIGO 2022 Diabetes in CKD (Rec 1.2.1, 1.4.1); RENAAL, IDNT; FIDELIO-DKD (NEJM 2020); FIGARO-DKD. Status: verify.

- ACE inhibitor or ARB recommended for diabetes with hypertension and albuminuria, titrated to the highest tolerated dose.
- Check potassium and creatinine 2-4 weeks after starting or increasing; a creatinine rise up to 30% can be acceptable.
- Never combine an ACE inhibitor with an ARB (higher risk of high potassium and acute kidney injury).
- Finerenone (non-steroidal MRA) for type 2 diabetes with UACR 30 mg/g or more, eGFR 25 or more and normal potassium (5.0 or below), on a maximum tolerated ACEi/ARB; kidney outcome HR about 0.82 in FIDELIO-DKD. Monitor potassium at 4 weeks.
- Potassium above 5.5 mmol/L: review ACEi/ARB/finerenone and diet.
