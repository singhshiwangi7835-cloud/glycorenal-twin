# Glucose targets and CGM metrics
Source: ADA Standards of Care 2026 (sec 6); International Consensus on Time in Range (Battelino, Diabetes Care 2019); Bergenstal 2018 (GMI). Status: verify.

- HbA1c target for most adults with type 2 diabetes: below 7%; less strict (below 8%) for older adults, those with frequent hypoglycaemia or advanced CKD.
- CGM targets: time in range 70-180 mg/dL above 70% of the day; time below 70 mg/dL under 4%; time below 54 mg/dL under 1%; time above 180 under 25%; glucose variability (CV) 36% or less.
- Each 10% gain in time in range corresponds roughly to a 0.5-0.8% fall in HbA1c.
- GMI (glucose management indicator) = 3.31 + 0.02392 x mean glucose in mg/dL; it estimates HbA1c from 14 days of CGM with at least 70% of readings captured.
- In advanced CKD, HbA1c can be less reliable (anaemia, shorter red-cell life), so CGM metrics help.
